Raw research

Five parallel web crawls run 2026-08-23. Every URL cited in these reports was loaded and inspected directly; sites that 404'd, 403'd, or turned out to be link-in-bio pages are reported as such rather than quietly dropped.

Roughly 80 distinct personal sites after de-duplication across lanes.

Known gaps in the research

  • reddit.com and quantnet.com were inaccessible to the crawlers, so no forum opinion is cited anywhere. The hiring-side lane is weighted toward firm-official statements and named practitioners instead, which is the stronger evidence base but a narrower one.
  • Gordon Ritter's page (cims.nyu.edu/~ritter/) returned 403 on every attempt and was excluded rather than reported second-hand.
  • A few reference sites are dormant (2–3.5 years stale) or dead. Those are reported as cautionary findings, not as models — see the dead-domain note in research/05.
